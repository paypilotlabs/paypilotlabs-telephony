# Code of Conduct

PayPilotLabs Telephony follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, welcome newcomers, give and accept constructive feedback, and never harass anyone. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions and comments that break these rules, and may ban people who do so repeatedly.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers through https://paypilotlabs.com/roadmap/. Reports are handled confidentially.
